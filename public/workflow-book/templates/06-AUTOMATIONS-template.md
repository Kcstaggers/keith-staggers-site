## First automation rule

Start with a read-only check that produces a useful internal record. Do not begin with publishing, sending, spending, deleting, or changing another person's account.

## Scheduled does not mean completed

A schedule proves only that the work was placed on a clock. The success record must prove that the intended result happened.
